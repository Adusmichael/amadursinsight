AS-IS vs TO-BE Workflow

The current manual cycle from a raw stakeholder ask to an approved backlog item, redesigned with an explicit AI/Digital Automation lane and a Human BA Checkpoint lane. Every arrow that crosses from automation into the checkpoint lane is a place a human must act before work continues — the mechanism detailed in the Safeguard Protocol.

AS-IS The manual cycle today

Three lanes. Most of the width belongs to one person doing everything by hand — and conflicts surface late enough to force rework.

TO-BE AI-augmented, with a human gate

Four lanes. The automation lane drafts in seconds; nothing reaches the backlog without passing through the checkpoint lane — the sign-off gate is the Safeguard Protocol made visible.
