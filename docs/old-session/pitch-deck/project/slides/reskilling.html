The BA role evolves. It doesn't disappear.

The reclaimed 60–70% moves to the three things AI still can't do.

was: manual documenter

AI Requirements Architect

Designs the schema and prompt rules — owns what "good output" means for the whole team

was: story writer

Strategic Facilitator

Runs the room and resolves the Conflicts an AI draft surfaces — a job with a named artifact now

was: format checker

Quality Governor

Catches hallucinated edge cases against real domain knowledge, owns the sign-off record

The draft was never the valuable part of the job. The judgment about whether the draft is right was — and that judgment is the whole job now, for juniors and seniors alike.
