BA Reskilling Brief

Generative AI now drafts BRDs and Given/When/Then stories in seconds — work that consumed 60–70% of a Junior or Mid-level BA's time. That time doesn't disappear. It moves to the three things AI still can't do: designing the extraction contract, facilitating the humans, and deciding what's good enough to ship.

60–70% of BA time was manual drafting 0 of that time is reclaimed automatically — it has to be redeployed on purpose 3 new roles replace 1 old one

Where the hours go instead

was: manual documenter
AI Requirements Architect

Designs the extraction contract — the schema, the prompt rules, what counts as a well-formed requirement — instead of typing every BRD by hand. Owns what "good output" means for the whole team, not just their own drafts.

was: story writer
Strategic Facilitator

Spends the reclaimed hours in the room, not at the keyboard: running alignment conversations, and now specifically resolving the Conflicts an AI draft surfaces — a facilitation job that didn't used to have a named artifact to work from.

was: format checker
Quality Governor

Reviews AI drafts against real domain knowledge, catches hallucinated edge cases and missed non-functional requirements, and owns the sign-off record — the accountability an unreviewed AI draft doesn't have on its own.

The skill shift, concretely

Goes from being the jobTo being the check
Writing Given/When/Then by hand→Judging whether generated criteria actually verify the story
Typing up meeting notes into a BRD→Designing what the extraction should classify and why
Noticing a stakeholder contradiction mid-meeting→Owning the resolution once a contradiction is surfaced for you
Reformatting documents to house style→Setting the house style as a schema, once, for every future draft

What changes for each of you

Junior BAJob redundancy fear is the wrong frame: the routine 70% moves to you as a review skill, not away from you as a task. Line-by-line verification against source notes is now the entry-level craft.
Senior BALess time rescuing bad first drafts, more time on domain architecture and the Conflicts queue — the part of the job AI hands you instead of taking from you.
BA Practice LeadGovernance shifts from reviewing documents to reviewing the prompt and schema that produce them — one artifact to govern instead of hundreds of drafts.

AI redefines the BA profession; it doesn't remove it. The draft was never the valuable part of the job — the judgment about whether the draft is right was. That judgment is the whole job now.
