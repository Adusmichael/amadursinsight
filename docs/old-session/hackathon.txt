1. Executive Summary & Challenge Statement 
Generative AI models can now instantly synthesize raw meeting transcripts, parse Jira 
backlogs, and write formal requirements documents (BRDs) with Given/When/Then user 
stories—tasks that traditionally consumed 60–70% of a Junior or Mid-level BA's workload. 
However, unvetted AI output introduces significant business risks: hallucinated edge cases, 
missed non-functional requirements, unaddressed stakeholder conflicts, and lack of 
accountability. 
Your Mission: As a Business Analyst, you must build a working prototype that automates 
first-draft requirements extraction while engineering a redesigned BA workflow. Your solution 
must establish explicit human-in-the-loop validation checkpoints, proving that AI 
redefines—rather than eliminates—the BA profession. 
2. Background & AS-IS Pain Points 
● Documentation Latency: BAs spend hours manually translating raw, ambiguous 
meeting notes into formal BRDs and Jira user stories, delaying sprint start dates. 
● Conflicting Requirements: Stakeholders often express contradictory needs during calls 
that BAs must manually spot and resolve. 
● Rework & Scope Creep: Misinterpreting stakeholder intent leads to incomplete 
acceptance criteria and costly late-stage development changes. 
● Administrative Burnout: BAs spend more time formatting documents and writing 
stories than facilitating strategic conversations or solving business problems.

 as input. 
● Auto-generate a structured Draft BRD containing business goals, functional

● Output at least 5 user stories formatted in standard Given / When / Then acceptance

Task B: BA Workflow Redesign (AS-IS vs. TO-BE) 
● Map the AS-IS BA Process: Show the current time-consuming, manual steps from initial 
meeting to approved backlog item. 
● Map the TO-BE AI-Augmented Process: Introduce a dedicated AI/Digital Automation 
Lane alongside a Human BA Checkpoint Lane. 
● Show clearly where AI generates the first draft and where the human BA reviews, 
validates, and refines. 

Task C: Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard Engineering 
● Define at least one explicit Safeguard Protocol preventing unverified AI output from 
moving directly to development. 
● Detail mechanisms for detecting AI hallucinations, resolving conflicting stakeholder 
requirements from logs, and ensuring compliance. 
Task D: One-Page BA Reskilling Brief 
● Formulate a strategic 1-page recommendation detailing how the BA skill set must 
evolve. 
● Highlight the transition from Manual Documenter & Story Writer to AI Requirements 
Architect, Strategic Facilitator, & Quality Governor.
