## Recording & export

- **Rehearse one generation first** so your words match what appears. Never fake output.
- Record face-cam parts in good front light, eye level, quiet room.
- Practise with a timer — if you run over 85 s, trim the 0:22 section first.
- **Export to stay under 10 MB:** MP4 (H.264), **720p**, 30 fps, video bitrate **~0.8 Mbps**, audio 96 kbps AAC.
  90 s at those settings ≈ 9.5 MB. If the file is over, drop to 0.7 Mbps.
- Free editors that do this: CapCut, Clipchamp (built into Windows), or HandBrake for re-compressing.
